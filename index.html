Why Universal

Built on trust.
Proven by results.

Caring staff, superior workmanship

Our foremen are professionally trained and highly approachable. They work with you to create the perfect landscape — we love what we do, and it shows in every project.

One call for all your landscaping needs

From planning to implementation, we do it all. No need to coordinate multiple contractors — Universal handles every aspect of your Halifax landscaping project.

No deposit required under $10K

We operate on trust and transparency. Projects under $10K require no upfront deposit, and for larger projects only a 50% deposit initiates the work — never full payment upfront.

Local Halifax knowledge and experience

We know the local climate, soil conditions, and how to build landscapes that withstand Halifax's ever-changing seasons. Established since 2022, deeply rooted in the community.

Fully insured for your peace of mind

We carry full liability insurance, so your property is protected on every job — from small cleanups to major hardscape builds.

Five-year workmanship warranty

Every eligible hardscape installation is backed by our five-year workmanship warranty, giving you complete confidence in your investment.
