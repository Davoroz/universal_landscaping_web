Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

Universal Landscaping ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you request a quote or contact us through this site, we collect the information you provide — such as your name, email address, phone number, property address, project details, and any photos you upload. We may also collect basic, non-identifying analytics about how visitors use the site.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote requests and inquiries.
  • To prepare estimates and schedule work.
  • To contact you about your project and our services.
  • To improve our website and services.

Third-party services

Our website uses a small number of third-party services, including Google Maps (to display our service area) and our website/form host. Form submissions are processed by our hosting provider and delivered to our team. These providers have their own privacy practices.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies necessary for the website to function and, where applicable, basic analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data retention & security

We keep your information only as long as needed to serve you and meet our legal obligations, and we take reasonable steps to protect it. We do not sell your personal information.
