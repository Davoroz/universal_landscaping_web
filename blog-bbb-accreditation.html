What BBB accreditation actually means

Not every business is a BBB Accredited Business. Accreditation isn't automatic and it isn't just a badge you buy — it's a commitment. To earn and keep it, a business has to meet the BBB's Standards for Trust, and accreditation can be revoked if those standards aren't upheld. More than 400,000 businesses across North America hold to these standards.

Here are the eight standards every BBB Accredited Business commits to:

Build Trust — maintain a positive track record in the marketplace.
Advertise Honestly — adhere to established advertising standards.
Tell the Truth — represent products and services honestly.
Be Transparent — openly disclose policies and guarantees.
Honour Promises — abide by all agreements and representations.
Be Responsive — address concerns quickly and professionally.
Safeguard Privacy — protect customer information.
Embody Integrity — approach every dealing with integrity.

Why it matters for our customers

Inviting a contractor onto your property is an act of trust. You're handing over your home, your budget, and your time. Accreditation is one more way to earn that trust before we've even met — here's how it works in your favour:

  • An independent reputation you can verify. Anyone can call themselves reliable. BBB accreditation means a neutral third party has reviewed our business, and consumers can check our profile on BBB.org to make an informed decision before hiring us.
  • Reviews and complaints that are real. BBB validates the identity of every person who leaves a review or complaint and confirms they actually dealt with the business. What you read on our profile comes from genuine customers, not anonymous strangers.
  • A fair path if something ever goes wrong. In the rare case of a disagreement, the BBB offers unbiased mediation and arbitration to help settle disputes — a fair, low-cost alternative to expensive litigation for everyone involved.
  • Accountability, in writing. By committing to the Standards for Trust, we've publicly promised to respond to concerns quickly and professionally. If we ever fall short, there's an independent body that holds us to it.
