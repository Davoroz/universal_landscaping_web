Driveways done right

Your driveway is the first thing people see — and one of the hardest-working surfaces on your property. At Universal Landscaping we design and install driveways in three proven materials, each with proper excavation, a compacted base, and drainage so they hold up beautifully through our winters.

Three driveway options

  • Interlocking pavers — premium looks, endless design and colour options, and individually repairable stones. The highest-end choice.
  • Poured concrete — clean, durable, and low-maintenance, with broom, stamped, or exposed-aggregate finishes available.
  • Gravel — cost-effective and quick-draining, with proper grading and edging for a tidy, long-lasting surface.

Built for our climate

A driveway is only as good as its base. Every install starts with proper excavation, a compacted gravel sub-base, and drainage, so your surface resists heaving, cracking, and settling through Nova Scotia’s freeze-thaw cycles — and stands up to snow, ice, and de-icing season after season.

The Universal Landscaping difference

From base prep to the final finish, we treat every driveway like it’s our own. Every hardscape installation is backed by our five-year workmanship warranty, giving you complete confidence in your investment.
