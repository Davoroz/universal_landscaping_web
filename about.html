Our story

Universal Landscaping began in 2022 as a small, hard-working crew in Bedford, Nova Scotia, with a simple belief: that quality work and honest relationships would speak for themselves. Word spread quickly, and what started with a few local yards has grown into a full-service landscaping, hardscaping, and construction company serving properties across the entire Halifax Regional Municipality.

Today our experienced team handles everything from complete landscape installs and driveways to concrete, carpentry, and winter snow & ice management — all in-house. But no matter how much we’ve grown, our founding principle hasn’t changed: treat every property like it’s our own, and every client like a neighbour.

Our mission

From the moment we break ground to the final finishing touches, our mission is simple: to transform ordinary properties into functional, beautiful, and long-lasting outdoor sanctuaries. We work with both residential and commercial clients across Bedford, Halifax, Hammonds Plains, Larry Uteck, and the surrounding HRM.

Whether it's a seasonal cleanup or a large-scale construction project, we offer full-service landscaping solutions designed to meet every need.

Our specialties

Our reputation is built on precision, creativity, and craftsmanship. Here's what we do best:

  • Paver installation
    Custom patios, walkways, and driveways built with premium interlocking pavers, designed for both beauty and durability.
  • Driveways
    Interlocking pavers, poured concrete, or gravel driveways — durable and built for our freeze-thaw climate.
  • Concrete
    Poured concrete driveways, patios, walkways, and steps, with stamped and decorative finishes.
  • Retaining walls
    Structural and decorative walls using Allan Block, Highland Stone, or Versa-Lok — engineered for stability, drainage, and style.
  • Sod installation & lawn replacement
    Complete site preparation, grading, and premium sod for an instant, lush, green lawn.
  • Grading & regrading
    Correcting slope, drainage, and soil levels to protect your property and ensure long-term landscape health.
  • Mulching & garden bed redesign
    Clean, polished beds with mulch, stone, or planting for year-round curb appeal.
  • Carpentry & fencing
    Chain-link, PVC, composite, and wood fencing — plus custom decks, gazebos, and pergolas.
  • Seasonal property maintenance
    Spring and fall cleanups, leaf removal, edging, pruning, and garden care.
  • Pressure washing
    Restoring the look of driveways, patios, decks, siding, and walkways.
  • Snow removal & ice control
    Reliable winter property care for both residential and commercial clients.
